            3. Setup
            3.1 DMX-512 Control Connection
            To connect the strobe light to a DMX controller, use a standard 3-pin XLR cable. Connect the XLR cable to the 3-pin XLR female output of your DMX controller and the other end to the 3-pin XLR male input of the strobe light. Multiple units can be chained together via a serial link. Ensure the required cable is a two-conductor shielded cable with XLR input and output connectors.
                Figure 4: DMX-512 Wiring Diagram
            3.2 Physical Installation
            The HYU-60PS is designed for self-contained installation. Use the provided bow mount and screws to securely attach the light to a truss, stand, or other suitable mounting surface. Ensure the mounting location can support the weight of the fixture and that it is positioned safely to avoid obstruction or accidental dislodgement.

            5. Maintenance
            Proper maintenance ensures the longevity and optimal performance of your HYULIGHTS HYU-60PS strobe light.
            5.1 Cleaning
                Regularly clean the exterior of the fixture with a soft, lint-free cloth.
                For stubborn dirt, use a slightly damp cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners or solvents that could damage the finish or internal components.
                Keep the LED surface free from dust and debris to maintain optimal light output.
            5.2 Inspection
                Periodically check all cables and connections for signs of wear, damage, or loose fittings.
                Ensure mounting hardware is secure and free from corrosion.
                Do not operate the device if any part is damaged.
            5.3 Storage
            When not in use, store the strobe light in a dry, dust-free environment. If storing for extended periods, consider using the original packaging or a protective case to prevent damage.
            6. Troubleshooting
            If you encounter issues with your HYULIGHTS HYU-60PS, refer to the following common problems and solutions:
                No Power: Ensure the power cable is securely connected to both the fixture and a working power outlet. Check the power source for functionality.
                No DMX Control: Verify that the DMX cable is correctly connected between the controller and the light. Check the DMX address setting on the light to ensure it matches your controller's output. Confirm the selected DMX channel mode is correct.
                Lights Not Responding: Check the control mode setting on the fixture. If in DMX mode, ensure the DMX signal is present and correctly configured. If in Auto or Sound mode, verify the settings for these modes.
                Inconsistent Light Output: Ensure all connections are secure. If using DMX, check for proper termination of the DMX chain.
            If the problem persists after attempting these solutions, please contact your dealer or HYULIGHTS customer support for further assistance.
